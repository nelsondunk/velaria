Control vocabulary
1 m-wide panel · 1280 px · 1 px ≈ 0.8 mm at 1.15 m · pinch jitter ≈ 8 mm
Ring · pan
You are the centre; front is up. Drag the dot around. Also fed by grabbing the card in the scene.
Used for: Yaw
Arc · tilt
Side view. Up is up. Half a ring, so it can't be confused with pan.
Used for: Pitch
Dial · angles
Pointer shows the angle. Drag up/down to turn it (not around the rim — that's the fatiguing one). Pinch the centre to zero it.
Used for: Roll, Object Spin / Turn / Lean. Glowing rim = being dragged (and raised in stereo).
Throttle · size, distance
Vertical, so "up = bigger / nearer" matches the hand. Log scale: the first half of the travel is the useful range.
Size 0.25–6×
Distance 0.5–20 m, nearer is up
Bar + nudges · amounts
Coarse by dragging, exact with the chips (one step per tap, hold to repeat). The readout is large and tabular.
Used for: Glow, Thickness, Corner radius, Window depth, Opacity
Switch · on/off
A 0/1 input is a switch, never a slider. Label says the "on" meaning.
Used for: Stereo footage, Frame on, Glow on
Wheel · colour
Hue around, saturation outward, brightness on a bar. Swatch shows the result; numbers underneath for matching.
Chips, tabs, readouts
One accent colour means "active". Pinch a readout to type a value on a number pad. Sizes: targets ≥ 60 px, labels ≥ 20 px, values ≥ 24 px.

Stereo in the interface. Each panel is already a real object at a real distance. Inside a panel, controls get elevation: idle 0, hover 2 mm, dragging 4 mm, pressed 0. That is a few pixels of per-eye offset in the render — enough to feel tactile, too little to blur text.
Material. Graphite panels, one warm copper accent, white readouts. No ornament inside the panels: the surreal flavour goes into the framing and the idle state (a soft glow pulse while Resolve re-renders), not into the controls you have to read.
What stays as it is. Look-down to open, grab-bar to move, pinch and trigger both work, Copy/Paste/Undo, keyframe diamonds. Every macro still auto-maps: unknown controls fall back to a bar.
