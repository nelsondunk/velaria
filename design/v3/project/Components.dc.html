Why 2.2 and not the 1.5 px in the system README: at 1.15 m a 1.5 px stroke is about one headset pixel and shimmers as your head moves. 2.2 on a 24 grid drawn at 28–36 px lands at 2.5–3 px. Line icons only, rounded joins, chalk by default, copper when the chip is on.
